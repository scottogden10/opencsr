## 9.3 Analysis Data Cutoff

The primary analysis data cutoff date is 01 May 2026. All efficacy and
safety summaries for the primary clinical study report are based on data
collected on or before this date.

## 9.4 Analysis Populations

Intent-to-Treat (ITT) Population: all enrolled subjects, analyzed according
to assigned treatment arm.

Safety Population: all subjects who received at least one dose of
examplinib, analyzed according to treatment received.

Response-Evaluable Population: all subjects who received at least one dose
of examplinib and had measurable disease at baseline per RECIST v1.1. The
Response-Evaluable Population is the primary analysis population for the
primary efficacy endpoint.

## 9.7 Statistical Methods

Continuous variables are summarized with descriptive statistics; categorical
variables with counts and percentages. Percentages are reported to one
decimal place with explicit numerators and denominators. No formal
hypothesis testing between arms is planned; the study is descriptive.

## 9.7.1 Primary Efficacy Analysis

The primary endpoint, confirmed objective response rate (ORR), is defined as
the proportion of subjects in the Response-Evaluable Population whose best
confirmed overall response is CR or PR per RECIST v1.1 (investigator
assessment). Confirmation requires a subsequent assessment at least 28 days
after the initial documentation of response. The confirmed ORR and its
two-sided 95% exact (Clopper-Pearson) confidence interval will be presented
by treatment arm. Best confirmed overall response categories (CR, PR, SD,
PD, NE) will be tabulated by arm. The primary efficacy output is
Table 14.2.1.

## 9.7.2 Sensitivity Analyses

Unconfirmed (best overall response) ORR will be summarized as a sensitivity
analysis. An ITT analysis of confirmed ORR, counting non-evaluable subjects
as non-responders, will also be presented.

## 9.8 Safety Analyses

Safety analyses are performed on the Safety Population. Treatment-emergent
adverse events (TEAEs) are adverse events with onset on or after the first
dose of examplinib. TEAEs are summarized by MedDRA preferred term and system
organ class, by worst CTCAE grade, seriousness, relationship, and action
taken. The adverse event overview is Table 14.3.1. Narratives are provided
for all deaths and all serious adverse events per ICH E3 Section 12.3.2.

## 9.9 Interim Analyses

No interim analysis is planned.
