## 9.1 Overall Study Design

OCS-101 is a Phase 2, open-label, multicenter, two-arm study of examplinib
in adults with advanced solid tumors harboring EXM1 activating mutations.
Approximately 80 subjects were planned, allocated 1:1 to examplinib 100 mg
QD or 50 mg QD in continuous 28-day cycles. Tumor assessments were performed
every 6 weeks per RECIST v1.1.
