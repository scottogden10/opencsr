# Protocol OCS-101 (EMBER-1) — Version 2.0 (Amendment 1), 14 Jan 2025

A Phase 2, Open-Label, Two-Arm Study of Examplinib in Adults with Advanced
Solid Tumors Harboring EXM1 Activating Mutations.

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — fictional study, fictional drug, generated data.

## 1.1 Background

Examplinib is an oral, selective small-molecule inhibitor of the EXM1 kinase.
Nonclinical data support activity in EXM1-mutant tumor models. Identified
risks of the class include gastrointestinal toxicity, hepatic transaminase
elevation, and pneumonitis.

## 3.1 Primary Objective

To evaluate the antitumor activity of examplinib in subjects with advanced
solid tumors harboring EXM1 activating mutations, as measured by confirmed
objective response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1 based on investigator
assessment.

## 3.2 Secondary Objectives

To evaluate duration of response, disease control rate, progression-free
survival, and the safety and tolerability of examplinib.

## 4.1 Study Design

Open-label, multicenter, two-arm study. Approximately 80 subjects will be
enrolled and allocated 1:1 to examplinib 100 mg once daily (QD) or examplinib
50 mg QD, administered in continuous 28-day cycles until disease progression,
unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal. Tumor assessments are performed every
6 weeks (±7 days) from the first dose.

## 5.1 Study Population

Adults (≥18 years) with histologically confirmed advanced or metastatic solid
tumors harboring an EXM1 activating mutation, measurable disease per RECIST
v1.1, ECOG performance status 0–1, and adequate organ function.

## 6.1 Primary Endpoint

Confirmed objective response rate (ORR), defined as the proportion of
subjects with a best confirmed overall response of complete response (CR) or
partial response (PR) per RECIST v1.1, as assessed by the investigator.
Responses must be confirmed by a subsequent assessment at least 28 days after
the initial response.

## 6.2 Secondary Endpoints

Duration of response (DOR); disease control rate (DCR: CR + PR + SD);
progression-free survival (PFS); incidence and severity of adverse events
graded per NCI CTCAE v5.0.

## 9.1 Statistical Considerations

The statistical analysis is specified in a separate Statistical Analysis
Plan (SAP), which is finalized prior to database lock. In the event of any
inconsistency between this protocol and the SAP regarding the operational
definition of an analysis, the approved SAP governs.

## 10.1 Safety Reporting

Adverse events are coded using MedDRA and graded per NCI CTCAE v5.0. Serious
adverse events are reported to the sponsor within 24 hours of awareness.
Pneumonitis is an adverse event of special interest and mandates dose
interruption pending evaluation.
